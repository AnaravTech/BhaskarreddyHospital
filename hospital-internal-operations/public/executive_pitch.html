⚡ Feature-by-Feature Executive Overview

Translating high-tech capabilities into clear, high-impact clinical and business advantages.

EXECUTIVE CONTROL 01

👑 Executive Command Center (CEO / MD Dashboard)

Real-time revenue telemetry (₹4.28L daily), bed occupancy gauges (85% ICU alert), doctor productivity leaderboard, and clinical alerts on a single, Bloomberg-style dashboard.

Business Impact: Eliminates daily manual reporting. CEO sees revenue split & bed capacity live.
CLINICAL OPD 02

🩺 Clinical OPD Workbench & 30-Sec e-Rx

Fast doctor consultation workbench with live token queue, vitals entry with BMI auto-calc, symptom chip selection, SOAP notes, and autocomplete e-prescription writer.

Business Impact: Doctors save 3 mins per consultation; auto-enforces 15-day follow-up validity.
WARD NURSING 03

🏥 Bedside Nursing MAR & 24-Hr Fluid Balance

Shift-wise Medication Administration Record (MAR) with nurse signatures, 24-hr Intake vs. Output fluid balance (+/- mL), Morse Fall Risk (0-45+ pts), and Braden Ulcer Scores.

Business Impact: Zero medication errors in wards; clinical fall and bed-sore prevention.
TRAUMA & LEGAL 04

🚨 Emergency Command & Medico-Legal (MLC) Desk

Red/Yellow/Green triage queue with animated pulse alerts, 1-click police intimation form generator on hospital letterhead, sample chain of custody, and resuscitation cards.

Business Impact: 100% statutory compliance for accident/trauma cases without administrative delays.
SURGICAL SUITE 05

✂️ Operation Theatre & WHO Surgical Safety Suite

Live OT-1, OT-2, OT-3 room monitors with elapsed time progress bars, interactive 3-phase WHO Surgical Safety Checklist (Sign In, Time Out, Sign Out), and PACU Aldrete Evaluator (0-10).

Business Impact: Apollo-grade surgical safety verification; objective PACU recovery discharge scoring.
MEDICAL RECORDS 06

👤 Patient 360° Unified Medical Record

Epic-style 7-tab chart: Demographics → Chronological Event Feed → OPD Consultations → IPD Admissions → Billing History → Insurance Claims → Digital Document Vault.

Business Impact: Lifetime medical record accessible in 2 clicks for returning patients.
GOVERNANCE 07

🛡️ Enterprise Workflow Approvals & Discount Control

Multi-level digital authorization engine for billing discounts, refunds, bed transfers, and emergency admissions with mandatory justification reasons and digital signatures.

Business Impact: Stops cash counter revenue leakage; full digital audit trail for management concessions.
FINANCIAL ENGINE 08

🧾 Billing & Multi-Payment Split Engine

Multi-channel payment split engine allowing single bills to be paid via Cash + UPI/GPay + Card + TPA Insurance. Generates itemized receipts with audit timestamps.

Business Impact: Fast cashier desk operations; 100% match between cash drawer and online receipts.
